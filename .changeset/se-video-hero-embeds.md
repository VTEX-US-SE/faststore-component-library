`SeVideoHero` takes Vimeo and YouTube links in `video.src` and `video.mobileSrc`, played in the provider's muted background player, sized to cover the frame and paused through its player API. `@vtex-us-se/components` adds `parseSeVideoHeroSource`, `seVideoHeroEmbedUrl` and `seVideoHeroEmbedCommand`; `useSeVideoHeroPlayback` now takes `mobileSrc` and returns the active source and embed state.
